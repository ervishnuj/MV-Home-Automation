Frequently Asked Questions

Can existing switches and wiring support smart devices?

Many homes can use modular smart switches if the switchboard has adequate depth and a neutral wire is available. Some solutions work without neutral. A site survey is the only reliable way to confirm feasibility for your specific boards and loads.

Do I need internet for everything to work?

Local control (touch switches, physical buttons) usually works without internet. Remote access, notifications and cloud-based scenes require a stable internet connection. We design so essential functions remain usable offline where the hardware allows.

Is home automation only for new constructions?

No. Retrofits are common. Success depends on existing electrical conditions. New construction simply gives more flexibility for clean cabling and future expansion.

Which brands do you support?

We work with selected reliable brands and modules that we can source and support. We do not claim compatibility with every device on the market. During consultation we recommend options suited to your requirements and budget.

How long does a typical installation take?

A single-room or few-switch upgrade may be completed in a day or two. Whole-home projects with curtains, multiple scenes and security can take several days to a couple of weeks depending on scope and site readiness. Timeline is confirmed after survey.

What about warranty and after-sales support?

Product warranties follow manufacturer terms. Installation workmanship support is provided as defined in the proposal. Specific warranty and support policy details are shared in writing before you approve the project. Placeholders only until finalised for your business.
